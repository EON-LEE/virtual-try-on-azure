PREPARATION AND LIMITATIONS
The bundled images were EXIF-oriented, resized without upscaling to a maximum
1280px edge, JPEG re-encoded and stripped of metadata. No crop, color correction,
retouching or synthesis was applied. Source bytes and bundle bytes have separate
hashes. "sourceDimensions" describes the downloaded display photo/thumbnail,
not necessarily the photographer's full-resolution original.

person-seated shows no lower legs/shoes: upper mode only. Other people show
full bodies but were not tested with upper/lower/full inference. upper-stripe
is folded, so sleeve length and full cut are not supplied by that reference.
lower-skirt is partly occluded by its tied sleeves. lower-shorts uses a 330px
public thumbnail: small lettering is not a reliable OCR/precision benchmark.
Do not claim material composition, physical fit, model identity or exact age.
