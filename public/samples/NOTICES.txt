비상업 연구 데모용이며 CC BY-NC 4.0 조건을 따릅니다.
상업적 이용을 허용하는 자료나 자유로운 실서비스용 사진으로 표시하지 않습니다.
샘플 원본은 수정하지 않았습니다. 화면에서는 축소·배치해 표시합니다.
생성 요청 시 별도로 EXIF 방향 보정, 메타데이터 제거, PNG 변환을 합니다.
생성 결과는 변경된 자료이며 원본 사진이나 검증된 착용 결과가 아닙니다.

PROVENANCE AND LIMITATIONS
Files were retained from the TryOnVirtual/VITON-HD-TEST mirror at revision
fbec00f2f68cacbcdd7e1dee047405e7319226c6. Their original bytes and hashes are
recorded in the authenticated /api/samples/manifest response. Mirror files have
not been independently compared with the official archive. Individual model
releases, photographer/catalog rights and age records are not independently
verified. No trademark endorsement is claimed.
All 14 hashes were checked against 77 excluded image/artifact hashes.

These are upper-body dataset inputs, not full-body lower-garment references.
Lower/full-set modes require the user's own suitable full-body photograph
and lower-garment upload. The application retains those modes.

ACCESS
Sample photographs require a current application session and are not served
from public static image routes. test/test is an intentionally weak, public
demo login, not strong privacy or production access control. Authentication
